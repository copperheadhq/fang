# balanced_output: SBOA092B page 50, Balanced Output

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## dc_gains: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 1

  gain_plus             -1   claimed -1 (a_plus) +/- 0.1%, holds
  gain_minus             1   claimed 1 (a_minus) +/- 0.1%, holds
  gain_diff             -2   claimed -2 (a_diff) +/- 0.1%, holds

## sine_1v_peak: transient, `.tran 1u 3m`

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 1 1k)

  pp_diff             4 V   claimed 4 V (pp_per_peak) +/- 0.2%, holds
             4 E_I p-p for E_I = 1 V peak, so the volts read as pp_per_peak:
             the handbook's claim, with E_I read as the peak. Held to 0.2%
             for the sampled peaks.
  pp_plus             2 V   claimed 2 V +/- 0.2%, holds
             Against ground, E_O+ swings 2 E_I p-p, no more than the input.
  max_plus            1 V   not a claim
  max_minus           1 V   not a claim

## sine_10v_peak: transient, `.tran 1u 3m`

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 10 1k)

  pp_diff            40 V   claimed 40 V +/- 0.2%, holds
             40 V p-p across the two outputs while each stays inside +/-13.5
             V: more than the 27 V p-p one output can give. Held to 0.2%.
  pp_plus            20 V   claimed 20 V +/- 0.2%, holds
             Against ground, E_O+ swings 2 E_I p-p, no more than the input.
  max_plus           10 V   not a claim
  max_minus          10 V   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_in_return): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e_out_minus): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (e_out_plus): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP5 (e_out_return): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp_1): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V
  U2 (amp_2): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

7 of 7 claims hold.
