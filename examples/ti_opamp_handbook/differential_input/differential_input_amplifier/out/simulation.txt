# differential_input_amplifier: SBOA092B page 67, The Differential Input Amplifier

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## e1_alone: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 0

  gain_e1           -10   claimed -10 (a_e1) +/- 0.1%, holds

## e2_alone: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 0
  drive  e2: DC 1

  gain_e2            10   claimed 10 (a_e2) +/- 0.1%, holds

## difference: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 0.3
  drive  e2: DC 0.5

  e_o           2 V   claimed 2 V +/- 0.1%, holds
       (R_O/R1)(E2 - E1) = 10 x 0.2 V

## common_mode: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 1

  e_o     -1.676 fV   claimed 0 V +/- 100 uV, holds
       1 V on both inputs. With the ratios matched the cancellation does not
       depend on the open-loop gain, so what ngspice reads is its own
       rounding.

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e1): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e2): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

4 of 4 claims hold.
