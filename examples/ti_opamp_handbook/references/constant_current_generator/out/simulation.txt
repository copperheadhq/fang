# constant_current_generator: SBOA092B page 51, Constant Current Generator

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## inside_limit: operating point, `.op`

  i_over_vz_r2             1   claimed 1 +/- 0.01%, holds
                I = V_Z / R_2 against the zener voltage the run measured:
                the op amp adds nothing to it.
  i_load            20.09 mA   claimed 20 mA (i_out) +/- 1%, holds
                Held to 1%, not 0.1%: the current is only as good as V_Z,
                and the zener model sits about 0.5% above 6 V at 7.3 mA. A
                real 6 V zener is a 5% part.
  i_zener           7.096 mA   claimed 7.273 mA (i_zener) +/- 5%, holds
                Held to 5%: every 10 mV of V_Z moves it by 64 uA. The
                printed 25 mA is not what 330 Ohm gives.
  v_zener            6.028 V   not a claim
  e_out             -10.05 V   not a claim

## past_limit: operating point, `.op`

  set    r_load.resistance = 800

  e_out               -13.51 V   claimed -13.5 V +/- 0.1%, holds
                  Not a handbook claim: at 800 Ohm, past the 675 Ohm limit,
                  the output is pinned at the -13.5 V swing. So 675 Ohm is
                  the largest load, not the smallest.
  i_over_divider             1   claimed 1 +/- 0.1%, holds
                  With the output pinned the loop is open, and the summing
                  point is no longer at ground: R_2 and R_L are a plain
                  divider from V_Z to -13.5 V, and I is (V_Z + 13.5 V) /
                  (R_2 + R_L), about 17.8 mA.
  i_load              17.77 mA   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (zener): zener model breaking down at 6 V
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (r_load): 500 Ohm split at the wiper, set to 1 of its travel
  TP1 (load_bottom): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (load_top): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

5 of 5 claims hold.
