# presettable_voltage_source: SBOA092B page 52, Presettable Voltage Source

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## ten_volts: operating point, `.op`

  e_out           10 V   claimed 10 V (e_out) +/- 0.01%, holds
          Held to 100 ppm: a noise gain near 10 costs 10 ppm of loop-gain
          error, and the 1 uV offset another 1 ppm.
  i_set           1 mA   claimed 1 mA (i_set) +/- 0.01%, holds
  i_cell  -1.002e-17 A   claimed 0 A (i_cell) +/- 1 nA, holds
          Held to 1 nA, absolute: the model's - input draws no bias current.

## five_volts: operating point, `.op`

  set    r_out.resistance = 3981.7

  e_out           5 V   claimed 5 V +/- 0.01%, holds
         The dial at 3981.7 Ohm: 3981.7 mV above Eref.

## dial_at_zero: operating point, `.op`

  set    r_out.resistance = 0

  e_out       1.018 V   claimed 1.018 V +/- 0.01%, holds
         The dial at zero: the lowest E_O the circuit gives is Eref.

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (r_out): 8.982 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 1 of its travel
  TP1 (out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (out_return): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 1 uV

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

5 of 5 claims hold.
