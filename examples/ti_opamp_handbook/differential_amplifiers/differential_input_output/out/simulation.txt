# differential_input_output: SBOA092B page 75, Differential Input-Output

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## difference: operating point, `.op`

E_O is the top output less the bottom one. Where the bottom output sits is
set inside the op amp; this model centres the pair on ground, so it is
-E_O/2 here and not a handbook claim.

  drive  e1: DC 0.1
  drive  e2: DC 0.4

  gain            10   claimed 10 (a_d) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_p         -1.5 V   not a claim

## floating_load: operating point, `.op`

The load (see `load`) is a card, since the figure draws none. The model's
outputs are ideal, so the load cannot pull them; a real part's output
resistance would.

  drive  e1: DC 0.1
  drive  e2: DC 0.4

  e_o              3 V   claimed 3 V +/- 0.1%, holds
          10 x 0.3 V, with 1 kOhm across the outputs and no ground on it
  i_load          3 mA   not a claim

## common_mode: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 1

  e_o      2.285 fV   claimed 0 V +/- 100 uV, holds
       1 V on both inputs gives no difference out

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (common): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e1): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e2): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (e_o): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP5 (e_p): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

3 of 3 claims hold.
