# common_mode_rejection: SBOA092B page 75, Common Mode Rejection

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## e1_alone: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 0

  gain_e1           -10   claimed -10 (a_e1) +/- 0.1%, holds
           on the reading that R1 is 10 kOhm; the figure's 1 kOhm would give
           a gain of 100 and no reachable null

## e2_alone: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 0
  drive  e2: DC 1

  gain_e2             10   claimed 10 (a_e2) +/- 0.1%, holds
            R0 / (R2 + 0.9 kOhm of R3), through the inverter
  inverted            -1   claimed -1 (a_inv) +/- 0.1%, holds

## difference: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 0.4
  drive  e2: DC 0.6

  e_o           2 V   claimed 2 V +/- 0.1%, holds
       10 (E2 - E1) = 10 x 0.2 V, as printed

## common_mode_trimmed: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 1

  e_o     -20.04 uV   claimed 0 V +/- 100 uV, holds
       1 V on both inputs with R3 trimmed so R2 + R3 = R1. What is left is
       the two op amps' finite gain

## common_mode_untrimmed: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 1
  set    r3.setting = 0.5

  e_o      -1.379 V   claimed -1.379 V +/- 0.1%, holds
       R3 at mid-travel: R2 + R3 = 11.6 kOhm, so E_O = -10 + 100/11.6 =
       -1.379 V for 1 V on both inputs. This is what the trim is for

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (r3): 5 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.82 of its travel
  TP1 (common): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e1): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e2): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp_invert): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V
  U2 (amp_sum): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

6 of 6 claims hold.
