## step: transient, `.tran 1u 5m`

A 1 V step at t = 0. The sketch has the output still until RC and then
moving; the simulated edge is smoother than that, already 10% of the way at
0.46 RC, but its middle sits at RC and it takes 1.1 RC from 10% to 90%.

  drive  e_in: PULSE(0 1 0 1n 1n 1 2)

  t50            589.1 us   claimed 600 us (rc) +/- 5%, holds
             the 50% point of the inverted step, against the RC the sketch
             marks; the page draws the delay and prints no formula, so the
             claim is held to 5%
  rise           671.2 us   claimed 660 us (rise) +/- 5%, holds
             10% to 90%, against the sketch's 1.1 RC, held to 5% for the
             same reason
  final              -1 V   claimed -1 V (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
             a 1 V step settles at -1 V
  t10            275.4 us   not a claim
  t90            946.6 us   not a claim
  overshoot      -1.022 V   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

4 of 4 claims hold.
