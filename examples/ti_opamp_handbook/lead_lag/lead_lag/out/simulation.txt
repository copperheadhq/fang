# lead_lag: SBOA092B page 86, Lead-Lag

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## dc_gain: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 1

  gain            -1   claimed -1 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
        inverting: the printed form has no minus sign

## response: ac, `.ac dec 200 100m 1k`

D1 = 1/2, D2 = 0.1. The spot frequencies stay below a few hundred hertz:
above that the wiper capacitor shorts most of the feedback and the op amp's
loop gain, not the network, sets the gain.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_low               1   claimed 1 +/- 0.1%, holds
  gain_100           2.742   claimed 2.741 +/- 0.2%, holds
              the transfer function at 100 Hz, 2.741, on its way to the
              2.778 (a_hf) it reaches only far above both corners
  phase_peak        -2.652   claimed -2.652 +/- 0.2%, holds
              radians: at 10.61 Hz, the geometric mean of the zero and the
              pole, the lead peaks at 28.1 degrees over the inversion's -180

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (pot_fb): 10 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.5 of its travel
  RV2 (pot_in): 10 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.1 of its travel
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

4 of 4 claims hold.
