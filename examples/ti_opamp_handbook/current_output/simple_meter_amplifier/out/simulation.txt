# simple_meter_amplifier: SBOA092B page 79, Simple Meter Amplifier

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## sine: transient, `.tran 10u 60m`

A 100 Hz sine of 1 V peak, four whole periods averaged. The average of |E_I|
is 2/pi of its peak, so the ratio is the meter's average current over the
input's rectified average. The 0.5% allowance covers the diode's turn-on at
each zero crossing, which the loop closes over but not instantly.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 1 100)

  g_meter        33.1 uA/V   claimed 33.1 uA/V (g_meter) +/- 0.5%, holds
  g_handbook     33.1 uA/V   claimed 33.33 uA/V (g_handbook) +/- 1%, holds
              the handbook's E_I / 30 mA, which the 100 Ohm meter takes 0.7%
              less than
  i_avg           21.07 uA   not a claim
  e_peak               1 V   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (d_bottom): 1N4148 diode model
  D2 (d_top): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  M1 (meter): meter movement of 100 Ohm
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

2 of 2 claims hold.
