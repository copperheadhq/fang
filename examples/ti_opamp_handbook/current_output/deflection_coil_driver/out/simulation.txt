# deflection_coil_driver: SBOA092B page 82, Deflection Coil Driver

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## dc: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 1

  i_per_volt   -100.1 mA/V   claimed -100.1 mA/V (i_per_volt) +/- 0.1%, holds
              The page prints -100 mA / Volt, the current through R3 alone;
              the coil also carries the E_I / R1 that R0 takes, 0.1 mA per
              volt more.
  e_out             -1.5 V   not a claim

## sine: transient, `.tran 0.5u 5m`

0.5 V peak at 1 kHz. The coil current is inverted, so it falls through zero
as the input rises through it; the lag is between the two crossings.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 0.5 1k)

  i_per_volt   -100.3 mA/V   claimed -100.1 mA/V (i_per_volt) +/- 0.5%, holds
              0.5% rather than 0.1%: at 1 kHz the op amp has 80 dB of gain
              left, and the coil's 63 Ohm of reactance against R3 divides
              what comes back, so the loop is a few hundred strong rather
              than a million
  lag                27 ns   claimed 0 s +/- 1 us, holds
              a voltage across the same coil would drive a current lagging
              85 degrees, 236 us at 1 kHz
  i_pp            100.3 mA   not a claim
  e_pp                 1 V   not a claim
  t_in                4 ms   not a claim
  t_coil              4 ms   not a claim
  out_peak         3.402 V   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

3 of 3 claims hold.
