# linear_current_source: SBOA092B page 81, Linear Current Source

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## r3_1000_ohm: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 1
  set    r3.resistance = 1000

  i_per_volt      999 uA/V   claimed 1 mA/V (i_per_volt) +/- 0.2%, holds
              Low by R_L / R2, 0.1% for 100 Ohm: the current the upper R2
              takes back.
  v_load           99.9 mV   not a claim
  second_out         1.1 V   not a claim

## step: transient, `.tran 0.5u 1m`

A 1 V step into E_I, to see the two loops settle rather than ring.

  drive  e_in: PULSE(0 1 10u 1u 1u 2m 4m)

  i_settled        999 uA   claimed 999 uA +/- 0.2%, holds
             1 mA per volt, less the 0.1% the upper R2 takes
  i_peak           999 uA   claimed 999 uA +/- 1%, holds
             no overshoot worth the name: the loop through the upper R2
             settles with C_O in place

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RS1 (r3): 1000 Ohm, with its current sensed
  RS2 (r_load): 100 Ohm, with its current sensed
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (first): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V
  U2 (second): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

5 of 5 claims hold.
