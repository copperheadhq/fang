# meter_amplifier: SBOA092B page 80, Meter Amplifier

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## sine: transient, `.tran 2u 200m`

100 mV rms at 1 kHz. The first 100 ms let the 10 uF bridge capacitors reach
their level; the next 100 are averaged.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 0.141421356 1k)

  reading_per_volt    4.479 mA/V   claimed 4.5 mA/V (reading_per_volt) +/- 1%, holds
                    The handbook prints 0.9 E_I / (R4 + R5), the full-wave
                    average of a sine over its rms. The drawn bridge has two
                    diodes and two capacitors, and its meter carries one
                    diode's average: 0.45. The 1% allowance covers R0 and
                    R8, which between them take 0.5% of the current.
  i_avg                 447.9 uA   not a claim
  e_rms                   100 mV   not a claim

## calibration: transient, `.tran 2u 200m`

R5 turned to its full 100 Ohm: the reading follows R4 + R5.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 0.141421356 1k)
  set    r5.setting = 0

  reading_per_volt    3.043 mA/V   claimed 3.061 mA/V +/- 1%, holds
                    0.45 / (47 + 100) Ohm, with the whole of R5 in circuit
  i_avg                 304.3 uA   not a claim
  e_rms                   100 mV   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (d_left): 1N4148 diode model
  D2 (d_right): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  M1 (meter): meter movement of 100 Ohm
  RV1 (r5): 100 Ohm split at the wiper, set to 0.47 of its travel
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

2 of 2 claims hold.
