# differentiator: SBOA092B page 61, Differentiators

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## derivative: ac, `.ac dec 100 1 1k`

Well below the peak the circuit is what the page says: a gain of 2 pi f R_O
C_I, 90 degrees behind the inversion.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  f_unity        15.92 Hz   claimed 15.92 Hz (f_unity) +/- 0.1%, holds
  gain_100          6.284   claimed 6.283 +/- 0.1%, holds
             2 pi x 100 Hz x 10 ms: the gain of a derivative rises with f
  phase_100        -1.571   claimed -1.571 +/- 0.1%, holds
             -pi/2 radians: -j 2 pi f R_O C_I, a derivative inverted

## peak: ac, `.ac lin 2401 12k 13.2k`

The handbook's Figure 43 point. The rising gain meets the 10 MHz op amp's
roll-off at sqrt(GBW f_unity) and the loop is left with almost no damping,
so a gain the page calls 2 pi f R_O C_I (793 there) peaks hundreds of times
higher. The height is the op amp model's, and is reported, not claimed.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  f_peak           12.62 kHz   claimed 12.62 kHz (f_peak) +/- 0.1%, holds
                Where the output's imaginary part changes sign, which for a
                resonance this narrow is the top of the peak.
  gain_peak        3.855e+05   not a claim
  gain_peak_db      111.7 dB   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (common): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

4 of 4 claims hold.
