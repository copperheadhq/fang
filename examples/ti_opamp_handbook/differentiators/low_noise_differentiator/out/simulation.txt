What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## response: ac, `.ac dec 200 1 1meg`

The second unity crossing, near 159 kHz, is where the falling gain passes 1
on its way down; it is not a claim of the page.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  f_unity           15.92 Hz   claimed 15.92 Hz (f_unity) +/- 0.1%, holds
  gain_corner             50   claimed 50 (a_peak) +/- 0.1%, holds
                Half of R_O/R_I: both poles at once, sqrt(2) each
  gain_peak               50   claimed 50 (a_peak) +/- 0.1%, holds
                and it is the top of the response: no plateau
  gain_decade          9.751   claimed 9.901 (a_decade) +/- 2%, holds
                1000/101: falling at 20 dB per decade, where the circuit
                with stop holds near 100. 2%: a decade up, the op amp's loop
                gain is only about 60 and takes 1.5% off.
  f_unity_high     156.6 kHz   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (common): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

4 of 4 claims hold.
