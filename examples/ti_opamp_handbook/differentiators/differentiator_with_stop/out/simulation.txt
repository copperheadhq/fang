# differentiator_with_stop: SBOA092B page 61, Differentiators, With "Stop"

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## response: ac, `.ac dec 200 1 1meg`

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  f_low             15.92 Hz   claimed 15.92 Hz (f_low) +/- 0.1%, holds
                The handbook prints 16 kHz; 1/(2 pi R_O C_I) is 15.9 Hz.
  gain_100             6.271   claimed 6.271 +/- 0.1%, holds
                100 Hz: 2 pi f R_O C_I = 6.283, less the first touch of R_I,
                1/sqrt(1 + (f/f_high)^2)
  f_high           1.567 kHz   claimed 1.592 kHz (f_high) +/- 2%, holds
                The handbook prints 0.6 kHz; 1/(2 pi R_I C_I) is 1.59 kHz.
                2%: the gain at the corner comes out 0.8% high (below), and
                on a slope of half a decade per decade that moves the 3 dB
                crossing about 1.6% lower.
  gain_f_high          71.27   claimed 70.71 (a_corner) +/- 1%, holds
                R_O/R_I over sqrt(2), 3 dB down at the corner. 1%: the loop
                gain there is only about 88, and with the op amp's 90 degree
                lag the finite gain lifts |E_O/E_I| by 0.8% rather than
                lowering it.
  gain_peak            99.97   claimed 100 (a_flat) +/- 0.5%, holds
                The top of the plateau, R_O/R_I. The op amp, closed for a
                noise gain of 101, rolls off near 100 kHz, only 60 times
                above f_high, so the plateau is a rounded top rather than a
                flat one.
  f_3db_op_amp     100.6 kHz   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (common): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

5 of 5 claims hold.
