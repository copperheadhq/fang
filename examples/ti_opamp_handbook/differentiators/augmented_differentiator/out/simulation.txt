# augmented_differentiator: SBOA092B page 62, Augmented Differentiator

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## input_term: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 1

  gain            -1   claimed -1 (a_dc) +/- 0.1%, holds
        At DC the derivative is zero and only -E_I is left

## both_terms: ac, `.ac dec 100 1 1k`

The page's E_O = -E_I - (1/100) dE_I/dt, in frequency terms: E_O/E_I = -(1 +
j 2 pi f x 0.01 s).

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_equal          1.414   claimed 1.414 +/- 0.1%, holds
               |1 + j| at f_equal: the two terms equal and 90 degrees apart
  phase_equal        -2.356   claimed -2.356 +/- 0.1%, holds
               -3 pi/4 radians: -(1 + j), inverted and 45 degrees ahead
  gain_159            10.05   claimed 10.05 +/- 0.1%, holds
               |1 + j 10| a decade up, where the derivative dominates
  gain_1              1.002   claimed 1.002 +/- 0.1%, holds
               |1 + j 2 pi 1 Hz 10 ms|: at 1 Hz, nearly E_I alone

## peak: ac, `.ac lin 2401 12k 13.2k`

Not a claim of the page. The derivative has no stop, so the noise gain rises
like the plain differentiator's and meets the 10 MHz op amp's roll-off near
sqrt(GBW x 15.9 Hz) = 12.6 kHz.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  f_peak           12.62 kHz   not a claim
  gain_peak_db      107.6 dB   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (common): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

5 of 5 claims hold.
