# simple_oscillator: SBOA092B page 83, Simple Oscillator

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## oscillation: transient, `.tran 2u 200m`

A 1 V kick on the 2C capacitor at the start, released at once, and 150 ms
for the oscillation to grow into the rails; the frequency is timed over the
forty rising zero crossings after.

  f_o              1.006 kHz   claimed 1 kHz (f_o) +/- 1%, holds
                1% for the trim: R/2 set 2% low moves the frequency at which
                the twin-T turns negative up by about 0.5%
  e_high             13.51 V   claimed 13.5 V +/- 1%, holds
                nothing in the loop limits the amplitude; the output clips
                at the op amp's swing
  e_low             -13.51 V   claimed -13.5 V +/- 1%, holds
  t_first           150.5 ms   not a claim
  t_last            190.2 ms   not a claim
  early_high         13.51 V   not a claim
  summing_peak      223.2 mV   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (r_half): 10 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.49 of its travel
  TP1 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

3 of 3 claims hold.
