# wien_bridge_oscillator: SBOA092B page 83, Wien Bridge Oscillator

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## as_drawn: transient, `.tran 5u 0.6`

The drawn circuit on the +/-13.5 V swing, tuned for 1 kHz. The lamp would
have to reach 705 Ohm, and a clipped output drives it nowhere near: the gain
stays far above 3, the output is a clipped wave, and its frequency is well
below 1 / (2 pi R C).

  e_high                13.51 V   claimed 13.5 V +/- 1%, holds
                   the output clips at the swing: the lamp has not warmed
                   enough to bring the gain down to 3
  e_low                -13.51 V   claimed -13.5 V +/- 1%, holds
  t_first              501.3 ms   not a claim
  t_last               532.8 ms   not a claim

  lamp_rms             508.7 mV   not a claim

## regulating_1khz: transient, `.tran 5u 1.2`

The same circuit with a +/-60 V swing, R at 10 kOhm. The oscillation grows
from a 0.1 V kick until the lamp warms to the gain-3 point.

  set    amp.output_high = 60
  set    amp.output_low = -60
  set    r_series.setting = 0.1
  set    r_shunt.setting = 0.1

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  LP1 (lamp): lamp: 70 Ohm cold, 714.3 Ohm at 10 V and 14 mA, heating with a 50 ms time constant
  RV1 (r1): 500 Ohm split at the wiper, set to 0.9 of its travel
  RV2 (r_series): 100 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.1 of its travel
  RV3 (r_shunt): 100 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.1 of its travel
  TP1 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

8 of 8 claims hold.
