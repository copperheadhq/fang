# simple_ac_amplifier: SBOA092B page 76, Simple Amplifier

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## response: ac, `.ac dec 40 0.1 10meg`

The -3 dB points are where the gain falls to 20 dB - 3.01 dB. The upper one
is not a handbook claim: it is the op amp's 10 MHz over a noise gain of 11.
At 1 Hz, a decade and more below the corner, the gain falls 20 dB per
decade.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_1k          9.999   claimed 10 +/- 0.1%, holds
            the magnitude of -10
  z_in           10 kOhm   claimed 10 kOhm (z_in) +/- 0.1%, holds
            the 1 V drive over the in-phase part of its current at 1 kHz,
            where C_I is 159 Ohm against 10 kOhm and moves it by 0.03%
  f_3db         15.92 Hz   claimed 16 Hz (f_low) +/- 1%, holds
            1/(2 pi R_I C_I) is 15.92 Hz; the handbook prints 16
  i_in_1k      -99.98 uA   not a claim
  gain_1hz        0.6271   not a claim
  f_high       909.1 kHz   not a claim

## dc: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 1

  e_out   5.049e-25 V   claimed 0 V +/- 1 uV, holds
         C_I blocks the 1 V: at d.c. the stage follows its + input, ground

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

4 of 4 claims hold.
