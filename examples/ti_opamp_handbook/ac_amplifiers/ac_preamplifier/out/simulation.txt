# ac_preamplifier: SBOA092B page 78, AC Preamplifier

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## response: ac, `.ac dec 200 0.001 10meg`

E_O is 3 dB down from 509.2 at 51.11 dB. z_in_100 is E_I over the current in
R_2 at 100 Hz, the bootstrapped input impedance the page calls high Z_in; it
is not a numeric claim of the handbook.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_100           509   claimed 509.2 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
            1 + 100k / (200 || 12.2k), read at 100 Hz, clear of the low
            corners and of the op amp's 20 kHz. The handbook prints 500;
            (R_0 + R_1) / R_1 is 501, and with the trim beside R_1 the gain
            is 509 to 546
  f_3db        809.5 mHz   claimed 800 mHz (f_low) +/- 2%, holds
            the handbook prints 1.6 Hz; 1/(2 pi R_1 C_1) is 0.80 Hz. The
            circuit, with its double pole and C_3 into 100 k, is 3 dB down
            at 0.81 Hz, hence 2%
  peak             756.9   claimed 757.7 +/- 0.5%, holds
            3.45 dB above the midband near 1.3 Hz, from the transfer
            function with R_1 C_1 = 0.2 s and R_2 C_2 = 0.1 s; the page's
            rule R_1 C_1 = R_2 C_2 would bring it down to 1.2 dB
  slope            40.85   claimed 40 +/- 1.5, holds
            dB per decade at the op amp's output between 0.1 Hz and 0.3 Hz:
            the double rolloff
  g_01             3.979   not a claim
  g_03             37.51   not a claim
  p_re                 1   not a claim
  p_im         8.261e-06   not a claim
  j_re            0.9995   not a claim
  j_im          0.003002   not a claim
  z_in_100    32.92 MOhm   not a claim
  f_upper      19.67 kHz   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (r_4): 10 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 1 of its travel
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

6 of 6 claims hold.
