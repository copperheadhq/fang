# single_supply: SBOA092B page 76, Single Supply

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## bias: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 0

  e_out          7.5 V   claimed 7.5 V (e_bias) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_plus         7.5 V   not a claim

## response: ac, `.ac dec 40 0.1 10meg`

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_1k         9.999   claimed 10 +/- 0.1%, holds
           the magnitude of -10
  f_3db        15.92 Hz   claimed 16 Hz (f_low) +/- 1%, holds
           1/(2 pi R_I C_I) is 15.92 Hz, as above; the handbook prints 16

## signal: transient, `.tran 2u 80m`

A 0.5 V, 1 kHz sine: the output swings 5 V either side of its 7.5 V rest,
inside the 0 V to 13.5 V it can reach. It is read over the last 5 ms of 80,
after the 10 ms C_I R_I transient of switching the sine on has died away.
The tolerance is 0.5% for the sampled peaks.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 0.5 1k)

  e_top           12.5 V   claimed 12.5 V +/- 0.5%, holds
            7.5 V + 10 x 0.5 V
  e_bottom       2.501 V   claimed 2.5 V +/- 0.5%, holds
            7.5 V - 10 x 0.5 V
  e_mean           7.5 V   claimed 7.5 V (e_bias) +/- 0.5%, holds

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output 0 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

6 of 6 claims hold.
