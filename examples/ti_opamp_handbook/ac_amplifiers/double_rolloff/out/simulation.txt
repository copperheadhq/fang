# double_rolloff: SBOA092B page 77, Double Rolloff

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## response: ac, `.ac dec 200 0.0001 10meg`

The sweep has 200 points a decade so the peak, near 0.52 Hz, is read to
better than 0.5%. The -3 dB point, where the gain first reaches 10 / sqrt 2
on the way up, is not a handbook claim.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_1k                10   claimed 10 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
  peak                29.21   claimed 29.21 +/- 0.5%, holds
               9.3 dB above the midband, from the transfer function with T_1
               = 1 s and T_2 = 0.1 s; the page's rule C_1 R_1 = C_2 R_2
               would hold it under 1 dB
  slope_steep         40.48   claimed 40 +/- 1, holds
               dB per decade between 0.05 Hz and 0.15 Hz: the double rolloff
  slope_low           21.22   claimed 20 +/- 1.5, holds
               dB per decade from 1 mHz to 10 mHz, below the zero at (1 +
               R_1/R_2) / (2 pi (R_0 + R_1) C_1) = 17.5 mHz, where the
               response is back to a single rolloff
  g_005              0.1056   not a claim
  g_015              0.9752   not a claim
  g_0001          0.0006923   not a claim
  g_001            0.007962   not a claim
  f_3db           330.9 mHz   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

5 of 5 claims hold.
