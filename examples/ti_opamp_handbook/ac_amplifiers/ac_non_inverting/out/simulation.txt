# ac_non_inverting: SBOA092B page 77, Non-Inverting

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## response: ac, `.ac dec 40 0.001 10meg`

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_1k                         10   claimed 10 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
  phase_1k                 0.0007348   claimed 0 +/- 0.01, holds
                        in radians: E_O in phase with E_I, as the page says
  f_3db                     1.608 Hz   claimed 1.6 Hz (f_low) +/- 1%, holds
                        the handbook prints 0.16 Hz; the drawn circuit is 3
                        dB down at 1.61 Hz, set by C_2 R_2 (1.59 Hz) with a
                        little from the gain network
  gain_network                 7.106   claimed 7.106 +/- 0.1%, holds
                        E_O over the + input at 1/(2 pi R_1 C_1) = 0.159 Hz:
                        sqrt(1 + 10^2) / sqrt(2), the gain network 3 dB down
                        from 10. This is the corner the page prints
  plus_at_input_corner        707 mV   claimed 707.1 mV +/- 0.1%, holds
                        the + input 3 dB down at 1/(2 pi R_2 C_2) = 1.59 Hz:
                        the corner that comes first
  out_at_gain_corner        707.1 mV   not a claim
  plus_at_gain_corner        99.5 mV   not a claim

## dc: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 1

  e_out   5.049e-25 V   claimed 0 V +/- 1 uV, holds
         C_2 blocks the 1 V; R_2 holds the + input at ground

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

6 of 6 claims hold.
