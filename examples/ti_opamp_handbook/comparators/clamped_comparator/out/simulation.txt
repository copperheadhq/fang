# clamped_comparator: SBOA092B page 47, Fully Clamped Voltage Comparator

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## above_threshold: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 3

  v_cr1      393.9 mV   claimed 394 mV (v_diode) +/- 1%, holds
         CR_1 carrying the summing point's 15 uA.
  e_out      -10.81 V   claimed -10.81 V (clamp_low) +/- 0.2%, holds
         The handbook prints -10 V (clamp_low_ideal); that is the clamp with
         a diode that drops nothing. With CR_1's 0.394 V and its 15 uA in
         R_b it is 0.8 V lower.

## below_threshold: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 0

  v_cr2      393.9 mV   claimed 394 mV (v_diode) +/- 1%, holds
         CR_2 carrying the 15 uA the reference pulls out of the summing
         point.
  e_out       3.518 V   claimed 3.518 V (clamp_high) +/- 0.2%, holds
         The handbook prints +3 V (clamp_high_ideal); with CR_2's drop and
         current it is 0.52 V higher.

## transfer: dc, `.dc VDRIVE_e_in -3 5 1m`

E_I swept from -3 V to 5 V. Between the clamps the loop has no feedback, so
the output crosses the whole 14 V within tens of microvolts of 1.5 V, what
the open-loop gain needs.

  drive  e_in: DC 0

  e_i_at_switch         1.5 V   claimed 1.5 V (threshold) +/- 0.1%, holds
                 Where the output crosses the middle of its two clamps. The
                 handbook's -(R_2 / R_1) V_ref would be 150 V; -(R_1 / R_2)
                 V_ref is the 1.5 V it prints.
  e_out_at_1v         3.428 V   not a claim
  e_out_at_2v        -10.62 V   not a claim

## figure_waveform: transient, `.tran 0.5u 2m`

The figure's own drive, a 2 V peak sine at 1 kHz. The figure's trace sits
near +3.7 V and -10.7 V, well off the printed +3 V and -10 V.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 2 1k)

  e_out_max       3.624 V   claimed 3.624 V +/- 0.5%, holds
             Not a handbook number. At the -2 V trough CR_2 carries 35 uA,
             not 15, and drops 0.432 V, so the same formula gives 3 + 1.2 x
             0.432 + 3 kOhm x 35 uA = 3.624 V.
  e_out_min      -10.62 V   claimed -10.62 V +/- 0.5%, holds
             At the +2 V crest CR_1 carries only 5 uA and drops 0.344 V: -10
             - (25/15) x 0.344 - 10 kOhm x 5 uA = -10.62 V.

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (cr_1): 1N4148 diode model
  D2 (cr_2): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_in_return): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (e_out_return): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP5 (ref): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

7 of 7 claims hold.
