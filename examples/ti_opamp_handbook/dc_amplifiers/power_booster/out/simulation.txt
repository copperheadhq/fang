# power_booster: SBOA092B page 72, Power Booster

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## thirty_volts: operating point, `.op`

E_I = 1.4286 V into a 10 Ohm load. The OPA277 alone would stop at 13 V; here
it swings 9.6 V while E_O reaches 30 V and 3 A.

  drive  e_in: DC 1.4286

  gain                 21   claimed 21 (g) +/- 0.1%, holds
             the OPA277's 20 uV offset, times 21, moves this by 0.001%
  e_out              30 V   claimed 30 V (e_out) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_front         9.598 V   claimed 9.6 V (e_front) +/- 0.5%, holds
             30 V x 4.7k / 14.7k is 9.592 V, plus the OPA512's 6 mV of
             offset, which its own input carries; the claim rounds to 9.6 V,
             hence 0.5%
  i_load              3 A   not a claim
  e_booster       30.15 V   not a claim

## offset: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 0

  v_os_out     -420.1 uV   claimed -420 uV (v_os_out) +/- 1%, holds
            21 x the OPA277's 20 uV, negative because the model subtracts
            its offset from the + input. The OPA512's 6 mV is inside the
            outer loop and divided by the OPA277's gain

## stability: ac, `.ac dec 40 10 100meg`

The -3 dB point is not a handbook claim; it is where the OPA277's 1 MHz,
over a noise gain of 21 and helped by the OPA512's 3.13, runs out.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_db         26.44   claimed 26.44 +/- 0.01, holds
           20 log 21
  peaking             0   claimed 0 +/- 1, holds
           no more than 1 dB of peak above the DC gain: the two loops
           together do not ring
  peak_db         26.44   not a claim
  f_3db         139 kHz   not a claim

## step: transient, `.tran 10n 40u`

A small step, so the missing slew limit does not matter.

  drive  e_in: PULSE(0 0.1 1u 10n 10n 100u 200u)

  final             2.1 V   claimed 2.1 V +/- 0.1%, holds
             21 x 0.1 V
  overshoot             0   claimed 0 +/- 0.05, holds
             a 0.1 V step settles with under 5% overshoot
  peak              2.1 V   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out_terminal): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (booster): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 4 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -35 V to 35 V, offset 6 mV
  U2 (front): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 1 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13 V to 13 V, offset 20 uV

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

8 of 8 claims hold.
