# simple_non_inverting: SBOA092B page 71, Simple Non-Inverting

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## gain: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 1

  gain               10   claimed 10 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_minus           1 V   claimed 1 V +/- 0.1%, holds
           the - input follows E_I: both inputs sit at the input voltage,
           which is the common-mode limit the page warns of

## swing: dc, `.dc VDRIVE_e_in -2 2 0.01`

A sweep of E_I from -2 V to 2 V. The output is linear at 10 E_I until it
meets the +/-13.5 V swing.

  drive  e_in: DC 0

  gain_at_1v              10   claimed 10 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
                E_O at E_I = 1 V, so the gain
  e_out_top          13.51 V   claimed 13.5 V +/- 1%, holds
                the macro-model's swing, reached at E_I = 1.35 V; its clamp
                diode lets it pass by a few mV, hence 1%
  e_out_bottom      -13.51 V   claimed -13.5 V +/- 1%, holds

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

5 of 5 claims hold.
