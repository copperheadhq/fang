# averager: SBOA092B page 65, Averager

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## average: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 2
  drive  e3: DC 4.5

  e_o            -2.5 V   claimed -2.5 V +/- 0.1%, holds
           -(1 + 2 + 4.5)/3 = -2.5 V
  divisor             3   claimed 3 (inputs) +/- 0.1%, holds
           -(E1 + E2 + E3)/E_O: the number of inputs

## unused_input_grounded: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 2
  drive  e3: DC 0

  e_o          -1 V   claimed -1 V +/- 0.1%, holds
       E3 grounded: -(1 + 2 + 0)/3 = -1 V. The scale is kept, and the
       grounded input counts as a zero in the average.

## unused_input_open: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 2

  e_o              -1 V   claimed -1 V +/- 0.1%, holds
           E3 left open gives the same -1 V: the open R_I sits at the
           summing point's ground and carries nothing. The page's advice
           does not change the scale of this circuit.
  e3_open      1.002 uV   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (common): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e1): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e2): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (e3): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP5 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

4 of 4 claims hold.
