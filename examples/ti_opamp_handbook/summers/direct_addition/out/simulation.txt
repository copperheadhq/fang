# direct_addition: SBOA092B page 65, Direct Addition

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## e1_alone: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 1
  drive  e2: DC 0

  gain_e1             1   claimed 1 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
  z_in_e1       15 kOhm   claimed 15 kOhm (z_in) +/- 0.1%, holds

## e2_alone: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e1: DC 0
  drive  e2: DC 1

  gain_e2             1   claimed 1 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
  z_in_e2       15 kOhm   claimed 15 kOhm (z_in) +/- 0.1%, holds

## both: operating point, `.op`

With E2 driven, E1's source no longer sees 15 kOhm: the + input sits at 1/3
V, so E1 drives (1.5 - 1/3) V across its 10 kOhm and sees 1.5 V / 116.7 uA =
12.86 kOhm. The page's 15 kOhm is for one input at a time.

  drive  e1: DC 1.5
  drive  e2: DC -0.5

  e_o               1 V   claimed 1 V +/- 0.1%, holds
           1.5 + (-0.5) = 1 V
  e_plus       333.3 mV   claimed 333.3 mV +/- 0.1%, holds
           (E1 + E2)/3: the + input at the average of E1, E2 and ground
  z_in_e1    12.86 kOhm   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (common): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e1): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e2): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

6 of 6 claims hold.
