# double_integrator: SBOA092B page 58, Double Integrator

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## drawn: ac, `.ac dec 20 1m 100`

The circuit as drawn: C_O = 1 uF, R_O = 10 kOhm. It integrates twice at low
frequency, with a coefficient of 50 rather than 4.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  k_low        -50.04 /s^2   claimed -50 /s^2 (k_drawn) +/- 0.2%, holds
              |E_O/E_I| times (2 pi f)^2 at 10 mHz, signed as the inversion
              makes it: the drawn circuit's low-frequency coefficient is
              -50, not the printed -4. The 0.2% band holds the -0.05% the
              input tee's pole still leaves at 10 mHz
  gain_0hz1          120.8   claimed 120.8 +/- 0.2%, holds
              from the drawn transfer function; the rule's formula gives
              10.13
  gain_1hz          0.3872   claimed 0.3872 +/- 0.2%, holds
              from the drawn transfer function; the rule's formula gives
              0.1013
  gain_10mhz     1.268e+04   not a claim

## rule: ac, `.ac dec 20 1m 100`

The same circuit with C_O = C_I/2 = 0.5 uF and R_O = R_I/2 = 500 kOhm,
written as cards beside the drawn one and read at its own output. |E_O/E_I|
(2 pi f)^2 is the coefficient, the same at every frequency.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  k_0hz1          -4 /s^2   claimed -4 /s^2 (k_rule) +/- 0.1%, holds
  k_1hz           -4 /s^2   claimed -4 /s^2 (k_rule) +/- 0.1%, holds
  phase_rad         4e-08   claimed 0 +/- 0.002, holds
             -4/(j 2 pi f R_I C_I)^2 is real and positive: two integrations
             lag 180 degrees and the inversion puts it back
  gain_0hz1         10.13   not a claim
  gain_1hz         0.1013   not a claim

## rule_step: transient, `.tran 1m 2.01`

A 0.1 V step at 10 ms into the rule's copy, from rest: -4 double integral
gives -0.2 t^2, -0.2 V at 1 s and -0.8 V at 2 s. The drawn circuit is in the
deck too, and saturates; it is not read.

  drive  e_in: PWL(0 0 10m 0 10.001m 0.1)

  k_step       -4 /s^2   claimed -4 /s^2 (k_rule) +/- 0.2%, holds
          E_O = -4 x 0.1 V x t^2/2, read 2 s after the step
  e_1s         -200 mV   not a claim
  e_2s         -800 mV   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+07 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

7 of 7 claims hold.
