# zeroed_integrator: SBOA092B page 56, Simple Integrators (offset-current null)

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## centred: transient, `.tran 1m 1.1`

Zero input, the reset switch open from 10 ms, and the wiper centred at 0 V:
the 1 mV offset drives 10.1 nA into C_O and the output drifts.

  drive  e_in: DC 0
  drive  rail_minus: DC -15
  drive  rail_plus: DC 15
  set    r3.setting = 0.5
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  drift      -10.1 mV/s   claimed -10.1 mV/s (drift_centred) +/- 0.5%, holds
           0.5%: the wiper's 7.5 kOhm source resistance is 0.08% of R5
  e_early     -1.909 mV   not a claim
  e_late      -12.01 mV   not a claim
  wiper       -749.4 nV   not a claim

## nulled: transient, `.tran 1m 1.1`

The same, with R3 at the setting the constraints solve for: the wiper at
-101 mV sends back through R5 what the offset takes through R1.

  drive  e_in: DC 0
  drive  rail_minus: DC -15
  drive  rail_plus: DC 15
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  drift     -7.483 uV/s   claimed 0 V/s +/- 100 uV/s, holds
           under 1% of the centred drift
  wiper       -100.9 mV   claimed -101 mV (v_wiper_null) +/- 0.5%, holds
  e_early     -1.001 mV   not a claim
  e_late      -1.008 mV   not a claim

## past_null: transient, `.tran 1m 1.1`

R3 turned past the null toward the - terminal: the wiper at -0.5 V over-
cancels and the drift reverses. dE_O/dt = -(Vos/R1 + (Vos + V_w)/R5)/C_O =
+39.9 mV/s.

  drive  e_in: DC 0
  drive  rail_minus: DC -15
  drive  rail_plus: DC 15
  set    r3.setting = 0.55
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  drift      39.86 mV/s   claimed 39.9 mV/s +/- 1%, holds
  e_early      2.588 mV   not a claim
  e_late       42.45 mV   not a claim
  wiper       -499.6 mV   not a claim

## short_of_null: transient, `.tran 1m 1.1`

R3 turned the other way: the wiper at +0.5 V adds to the offset's current.
dE_O/dt = -60.1 mV/s.

  drive  e_in: DC 0
  drive  rail_minus: DC -15
  drive  rail_plus: DC 15
  set    r3.setting = 0.45
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  drift     -60.06 mV/s   claimed -60.1 mV/s +/- 1%, holds
  e_early     -6.406 mV   not a claim
  e_late      -66.47 mV   not a claim
  wiper        499.6 mV   not a claim

## rate: transient, `.tran 1m 1.1`

Nulled, with 0.1 V on E_I: -10 V/s per volt, the rate with C_O read as 1 uF.
As printed, 1 mF would give -0.01 per second.

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (r3): 10 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.5101 of its travel
  SW1 (reset): switch: 1 Ohm closed, 1 TOhm open
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (rail_minus): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (rail_plus): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 1 mV

6 of 6 claims hold.
