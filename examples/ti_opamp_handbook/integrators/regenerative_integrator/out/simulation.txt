# regenerative_integrator: SBOA092B page 57, Integrator with zero control and regeneration

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## rate: transient, `.tran 1m 1.1`

0.1 V on E_I with R5 at a quarter; the reset switch opens at 10 ms.

  drive  e_in: DC 0.1
  drive  rail_minus: DC -15
  drive  rail_plus: DC 15
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  rate_per_volt     -9.995 /s   claimed -10 /s (rate) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_early           -89.99 mV   not a claim
  e_late              -1.09 V   not a claim

## hold_without_regeneration: transient, `.tran 10m 101`

Regeneration taken out: node X tied to ground by a card the bench adds, so
the + input sees none of the output. The input runs the output up to about
-5 V and is opened at 0.51 s; the output then leaks through R6 with the time
constant A R6 C_O.

  drive  rail_minus: DC -15
  drive  rail_plus: DC 15
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  tau         5.015 ks   claimed 5 ks (hold_open) +/- 3%, holds
          3%: A R6 C_O leaves out the zero control's source resistance and
          the path through R7 and R2 to the + input
  e_1s        -4.996 V   not a claim
  e_101s      -4.898 V   not a claim

## hold_with_regeneration: transient, `.tran 10m 101`

The circuit as drawn, R5 at a quarter of its travel: the same run up and the
same open input, and a hold three times longer.

  drive  rail_minus: DC -15
  drive  rail_plus: DC 15
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  tau         15.35 ks   claimed 15.71 ks (hold_quarter) +/- 3%, holds
          3%, for the same simplifications as the run above
  e_1s        -4.999 V   not a claim
  e_101s      -4.967 V   not a claim

## too_much_regeneration: transient, `.tran 10m 101`

R5 at the top of its travel: k = 2.7e-4 is past 1/A = 2e-4, so the output
grows (a negative time constant), the handbook's cue to decrease
regeneration. Not a claim: it shows the other side of the balance.

  drive  rail_minus: DC -15
  drive  rail_plus: DC 15
  set    r5.setting = 1
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  e_1s        -5.002 V   not a claim
  e_101s      -5.037 V   not a claim
  tau        -14.53 ks   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (r5): 2 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.25 of its travel
  RV2 (r8): 10 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.5 of its travel
  SW1 (reset): switch: 1 Ohm closed, 1 TOhm open
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (rail_minus): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (rail_plus): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 5000 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

3 of 3 claims hold.
