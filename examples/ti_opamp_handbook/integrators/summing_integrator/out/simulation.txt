# summing_integrator: SBOA092B page 58, Summing Integrator

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## sum: transient, `.tran 1m 1.1`

E1 + E2 + E3 = 0.6 V, so the output falls 6 V/s once the switch opens at 10
ms. The slope over 0.1 s to 1.1 s, divided by 0.6 V, is the rate.

  drive  e1: DC 0.1
  drive  e2: DC 0.2
  drive  e3: DC 0.3
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  rate_per_volt        -10 /s   claimed -10 /s (rate) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_early             -540 mV   not a claim
  e_late              -6.54 V   not a claim

## mixed_signs: transient, `.tran 1m 1.1`

E1 + E2 + E3 = 0.4 V with E2 negative: the output falls 4 V/s, the rate of
the algebraic sum.

  drive  e1: DC 0.5
  drive  e2: DC -0.2
  drive  e3: DC 0.1
  switch reset: PWL(0 1 10m 1 10.001m 0)

  rate_per_volt        -10 /s   claimed -10 /s (rate) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_early             -360 mV   not a claim
  e_late              -4.36 V   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  SW1 (reset): switch: 1 Ohm closed, 1 TOhm open
  TP1 (e1): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e2): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP3 (e3): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP4 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

2 of 2 claims hold.
