# ac_integrator: SBOA092B page 59, AC Integrator

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## dc: operating point, `.op`

E_I = 1 V DC. C_I has charged to -E_O, and the - input follows it to E_I.

  drive  e_in: DC 1

  dc_gain               -1   claimed -1 +/- 0.1%, holds
              a DC input comes out inverted, not integrated: nothing ramps
  plus_input           1 V   not a claim

## sine: ac, `.ac dec 50 1m 100k`

Above the 1.59 Hz corner the circuit integrates; below it the gain returns
to 1. The peak at the corner is the Q of 50 the drawn values give; it is not
a handbook claim.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_unity             1   claimed 1 +/- 0.1%, holds
              1/(2 pi f_unity R_I C_O) = 1
  gain_100hz         1.592   claimed 1.591 +/- 0.1%, holds
              1/(2 pi 100 Hz x 1 ms)
  phase_deg          90.01   claimed 90 +/- 0.1, holds
              an inverting integrator: the output leads the input by 90
              degrees
  gain_1mhz          1.002   claimed 1.002 +/- 0.1%, holds
              at 1 mHz the circuit is back to its DC gain of 1 (|1 + j 2 pi
              f tau_2| = 1.002)
  phase_rad          1.571   not a claim
  peak                4573   not a claim
  f_peak          1.585 Hz   not a claim

## ac_on_dc: transient, `.tran 10u 1.1`

0.1 V DC plus a 0.1 V, 100 Hz cosine. The capacitors start where the steady
state puts them at t = 0 (the `.ic`), so the 1.6 Hz resonance is barely
rung. The swing is read over 1.0 to 1.03 s; the average over 0.4 to 1.03 s,
63 cycles of the input and one period of that resonance.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0.1 0.1 100 0 0 90)

  e_pp       318.6 mV   claimed 318.3 mV +/- 1%, holds
         0.1 V at 100 Hz integrated: 2 x 0.1/(2 pi 100 Hz x 1 ms). 1%: the
         start is close to the steady state, not exactly on it, and what is
         left rings at 1.6 Hz under the 100 Hz wave
  e_avg       -100 mV   claimed -100 mV +/- 3 mV, holds
         the 0.1 V DC component passes at -1 and does not ramp

## dc_step: transient, `.tran 1m 100`

A 10 mV DC step at 10 ms, from rest. The output rings at 1.6 Hz (the Q of
50) and settles at -10 mV with a 10 s time constant; the swing it reaches
first is in e_min and e_max.

  drive  e_in: PWL(0 0 10m 0 10.001m 0.01)

  e_100s     -10.03 mV   claimed -10 mV +/- 500 uV, holds
          an ordinary integrator would be at -1000 V (a rail) by now; this
          one has settled at -E_I
  e_min      -994.2 mV   not a claim
  e_max       943.4 mV   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  SW1 (reset): switch: 1 Ohm closed, 1 TOhm open
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

8 of 8 claims hold.
