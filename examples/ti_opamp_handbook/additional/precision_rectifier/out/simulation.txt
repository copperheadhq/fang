# precision_rectifier: SBOA092B page 88, Precision Rectifier

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## transfer: dc, `.dc VDRIVE_e_in -2 2 0.01`

  drive  e_in: DC 0

  gain_negative            -5   claimed -5 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_at_plus_1       -19.69 uV   claimed 0 V +/- 1 mV, holds
                 the other half: the upper diode is off and E_O rests on the
                 virtual ground through R_O
  e_at_minus_1            5 V   not a claim

## half_wave: transient, `.tran 1u 3m`

E_I is a 1 V peak, 1 kHz sine; the last two cycles are measured. The one
excursion below zero is a spike of a few microseconds as E_I crosses zero
going positive, while the op amp output swings across the two diode drops
and the upper diode recovers. It is reported, not claimed.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 1 1k)

  e_peak             5 V   claimed 5 V +/- 0.2%, holds
            -5 times the -1 V peak of E_I
  e_off        -19.77 uV   claimed 0 V +/- 1 mV, holds
            at the positive peak of E_I: E_O stays at zero, without a diode
            drop
  e_glitch     -8.816 mV   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (d_clamp): 1N4148 diode model
  D2 (d_out): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

4 of 4 claims hold.
