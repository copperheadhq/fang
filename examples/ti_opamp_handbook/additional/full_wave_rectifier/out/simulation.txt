# full_wave_rectifier: SBOA092B page 89, Full Wave Rectifier

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## transfer: dc, `.dc VDRIVE_e_in -2 2 1m`

  drive  e_in: DC 0

  gain_positive            -1   claimed -1 (a_positive) +/- 0.1%, holds
  gain_negative             1   claimed 1 (a_negative) +/- 0.1%, holds
  e_plus_10m        -9.998 mV   claimed -10 mV +/- 100 uV, holds
                 10 mV in, -10 mV out, to 0.1 mV: no diode drop shows
  e_minus_10m       -9.993 mV   claimed -10 mV +/- 100 uV, holds
  e_plus_1               -1 V   not a claim
  e_minus_1              -1 V   not a claim

## sine: transient, `.tran 1u 3m`

E_I is a 1 V peak, 1 kHz sine; the last two cycles are measured.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 1 1k)

  e_min              -1 V   claimed -1 V +/- 0.2%, holds
             both peaks of the 1 V sine come out at -1 V
  e_average     -636.6 mV   claimed -636.6 mV +/- 0.2%, holds
             -2/pi of the peak, the mean of a full-wave rectified sine; the
             0.2% allows for the few microseconds at each zero crossing
             while the first op amp swings across its diodes

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (d_down): 1N4148 diode model
  D2 (d_up): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp_1): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V
  U2 (amp_2): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

6 of 6 claims hold.
