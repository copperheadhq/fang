# ac_to_dc_converter: SBOA092B page 88, AC to DC Converter

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## six_volts: transient, `.tran 50u 10`

E_I is 6 V rms (8.485 V peak) at 100 Hz, the top of the page's range. The
average is taken over the last second of ten.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 8.485281 100)

  k_measured           0.9   claimed 0.9 (k_avg) +/- 0.1%, holds
              the page's 0.9; the circuit gives 0.9003 at unity gain, and
              what is left of the filter's start-up after 9 s is e^-9, a
              hundredth of a percent
  e_average          5.4 V   not a claim
  ripple          6.104 mV   not a claim

## six_millivolts: transient, `.tran 50u 10`

E_I is 6 mV rms at 100 Hz, the bottom of the page's range, where a diode
drop outside the loop would swallow the signal whole. The average is taken
over the last second of ten.

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 8.485281m 100)

  k_measured        0.8941   claimed 0.9 (k_avg) +/- 1%, holds
              1% here: at 8.5 mV peak the signal current through R_1 is
              under 1 uA, and the 1N4148 model's 2.5 nA of saturation
              current through the diode that should be off, with the 4 pF
              junction charge moved at each zero crossing, takes a few
              tenths of a percent of it
  e_average       5.365 mV   not a claim
  ripple          6.148 uV   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (d_half): 1N4148 diode model
  D2 (d_return): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  RV1 (r_8): 2 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0 of its travel
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp_1): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V
  U2 (amp_2): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

2 of 2 claims hold.
