# time_delay_relay: SBOA092B page 90, Time Delay

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## k_tenth: transient, `.tran 1m 6`

The switch is open (reset) until 0.1 s and closed after. R_7 at K = 0.1.
Pull-in is the coil current crossing 4.5 mA, and the delay is counted from
the switch closing.

  set    r_7.setting = 0.1
  switch start: PWL(0 0 0.1 0 0.1001 1)

  delay_measured        4.753 s   claimed 5 s (delay) +/- 7%, holds
                   the page's R_1 C_0 / (2K) is 5 s. The circuit is 5% short
                   of it, as the start-up diode drop, the ramp ending at R_5
                   K E / R_1 and the wiper's 0.9 kOhm predict (4.75 s)
  coil_reset          -2.528 nA   claimed 0 A +/- 10 uA, holds
                   reset: the coil's diode is reverse biased by S at 7.8 V,
                   and nothing flows
  coil_peak_early      17.97 uA   claimed 0 A +/- 1 mA, holds
                   closing the switch does not itself pull the relay in: the
                   output leaves its clamp as the switch closes
  t_reset               7.215 V   not a claim
  t_pull_in             4.853 s   not a claim
  coil_on              7.013 mA   not a claim

## k_half: transient, `.tran 1m 1.5`

R_7 at K = 0.5, where the page's formula gives 1 s.

  set    r_7.setting = 0.5
  switch start: PWL(0 0 0.1 0 0.1001 1)

  delay_measured       886.4 ms   claimed 886 ms +/- 2%, holds
                   against the program's own estimate (`shortfall`), not the
                   page's 1 s: the ramp's end at R_5 K E / R_1 and the
                   wiper's 2.5 kOhm cost 11% here
  coil_peak_early      18.49 uA   claimed 0 A +/- 1 mA, holds
                   closing the switch does not itself pull the relay in: the
                   output leaves its clamp as the switch closes
  t_reset               7.215 V   not a claim
  coil_reset          -2.528 nA   not a claim
  t_pull_in            986.4 ms   not a claim
  coil_on              7.387 mA   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (d_clamp): 1N4148 diode model
  D2 (d_coil): 1N4148 diode model
  D3 (d_ramp): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  M1 (coil): meter movement of 1 kOhm
  RV1 (r_7): 10 kOhm split at the wiper, set to 0.1 of its travel
  SW1 (start): switch: 1 Ohm closed, 1 TOhm open
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

5 of 5 claims hold.
