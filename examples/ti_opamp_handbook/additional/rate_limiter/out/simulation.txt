# rate_limiter: SBOA092B page 89, Rate Limiter

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## settled: operating point, `.op`

  drive  e_in: DC 2

  gain            -1   claimed -1 (a_v) +/- 0.1%, holds

## small_step: transient, `.tran 100u 0.3`

E_I steps from 0 to 0.1 V at 0.1 s. At the limit that is a 14 ms ramp, so
E_O is settled well before 0.2 s.

  drive  e_in: PWL(0 0 0.1 0 0.1001 0.1)

  e_after        -100 mV   claimed -100 mV +/- 0.2%, holds
            -E_I for a 0.1 V step, averaged over the last 0.1 s
  e_before   -3.44e-18 V   not a claim
  t_half        106.9 ms   not a claim

## large_step: transient, `.tran 200u 1.2`

E_I steps from 0 to 5 V at 0.1 s. The slope is timed between E_O = -1 V and
-4 V, well clear of both ends of the ramp.

  drive  e_in: PWL(0 0 0.1 0 0.1001 5)

  slew          7.268 V/s   claimed 7.5 V/s (rate_limit) +/- 5%, holds
             the page's 7.5 V/s is 15 V / ((R_3 + R_5) C_0); the diode in
             the path takes about 0.45 V of the 15, so the circuit slews
             about 3% slower
  e_final            -5 V   claimed -5 V +/- 0.2%, holds
             and it arrives at -E_I
  t_minus_1      237.6 ms   not a claim
  t_minus_4      650.4 ms   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (d_bottom_left): 1N4148 diode model
  D2 (d_bottom_right): 1N4148 diode model
  D3 (d_top_left): 1N4148 diode model
  D4 (d_top_right): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp_1): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V
  U2 (amp_2): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

Coverage the plan itself says it lacks:

  an operating point says nothing about dynamic behaviour

4 of 4 claims hold.
