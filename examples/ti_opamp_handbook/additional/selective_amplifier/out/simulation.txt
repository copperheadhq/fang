# selective_amplifier: SBOA092B page 91, Selective Amplifier

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## around_the_peak: ac, `.ac lin 3001 900 1050`

A linear sweep, 0.05 Hz a step, from 900 Hz to 1050 Hz. The peak is sharp,
about 20 Hz wide, and sits a few hertz above the notch: there the twin-T's
transfer admittance has a negative real part that cancels part of R_O's
conductance, so the gain at the top is higher than R_O / |Z_in|: 44, where
the page says 33.

  drive  e_in: DC 0 AC 1

  gain_notch           31.66   claimed 31.34 (a_notch) +/- 2%, holds
                at the notch, 964.6 Hz: R_O over |Z_in|. The page's R_O /
                R_I = 33 leaves out C_I's 3.3 kOhm of reactance. 2%, because
                the gain climbs 1.6 per hertz here and the simulated notch
                sits about 0.2 Hz below 1/(2 pi R_a C_a)
  z_in_notch      10.52 kOhm   claimed 10.53 kOhm (z_in) +/- 0.5%, holds
                1 V over the current through R_I, at the notch. The page
                says Z_in = R_I = 10 kOhm, which leaves out C_I. 0.5%,
                because the current is read as the voltage at R_I's input
                end over 10 kOhm, and the summing point is a few millivolts
                off ground with 80 dB of open-loop gain at 1 kHz
  f_centre          973.9 Hz   claimed 964.6 Hz (f_notch) +/- 2%, holds
                the middle of the -3 dB band, 1% above the notch. The page
                prints 1000 Hz; 1/(2 pi R_a C_a) is 964.6 Hz
  v_r_i             950.5 mV   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

3 of 3 claims hold.
