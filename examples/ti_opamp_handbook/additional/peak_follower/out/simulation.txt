# peak_follower: SBOA092B page 87, Peak Follower

What ngspice measured, against what the program claims. The decks
it ran are beside this file, under spice/.

## pulse: transient, `.tran 1u 10m`

E_I pulses to 4 V from 1 ms to 2 ms and falls back to 0. The reset switch
closes at 7 ms.

  drive  e_in: PULSE(0 4 1m 1u 1u 1m 20m)
  switch reset: PWL(0 0 7m 0 7.001m 1)

  held             3.556 V   claimed 3.5 V (e_held) +/- 100 mV, holds
              3 ms after a 4 V, 1 ms pulse: 0.44 V under the peak, the drop
              at the 45 uA the capacitor still took when the pulse ended.
              The page's E_O = E_I maximum would be 4 V
  held_later       3.556 V   claimed 3.5 V (e_held) +/- 100 mV, holds
              still there 1.9 ms later: the model's capacitor does not leak
  cleared      4.931e-25 V   claimed 0 V +/- 1 mV, holds
              the reset switch closed at 7 ms

## sine: transient, `.tran 1u 10m`

  drive  e_in: SIN(0 4 1k)

  held             3.517 V   claimed 3.5 V (e_held) +/- 100 mV, holds
              after ten cycles of a 4 V, 1 kHz sine: 0.48 V under the peak.
              Each cycle tops the capacitor up in a short burst near the
              crest, which leaves a little more drop than the pulse did
  input_peak           4 V   not a claim

## What the plan abstracted, and what the bench wrote for it

fang lowers the resistors, capacitors and cells itself. These it names
as abstracted, which puts each in the plan's assumptions, and the bench
writes their cards instead:

  D1 (diode): 1N4148 diode model
  GND1 (ground): the ground; its net is node 0, and it adds nothing
  SW1 (reset): switch: 1 Ohm closed, 1 TOhm open
  TP1 (e_in): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  TP2 (e_out): a terminal; it marks a node and adds nothing
  U1 (amp): op amp macro-model: 1e+06 open-loop gain, 10 MHz gain-bandwidth, output -13.5 V to 13.5 V, offset 0 V

4 of 4 claims hold.
